Leave request approval:

Inputs: requested days (number), doctor note provided (boolean), is during blackout period (boolean).

Rules:
- Requests during blackout period are rejected with reason "blackout".
- Requests longer than 30 days are rejected with reason "too long".
- Requests of 3 or more days without a doctor note are rejected with reason "doctor note required".
- Everything else is approved with reason "ok".

Output: approved (boolean) and reason (text).
