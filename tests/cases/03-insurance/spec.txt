Car insurance acceptance decision:

Inputs: driver age, count of at-fault accidents in last 3 years.

Rules:
- Refuse any driver younger than 18: application_status "declined", premium_tier "none".
- Refuse any driver with more than 3 at-fault accidents: application_status "declined", premium_tier "none".
- Drivers aged 18 to 25 with at most 1 accident are accepted as premium_tier "high" with base premium 900.
- Drivers over 25 with no accidents are accepted as premium_tier "low" with base premium 400.
- All other accepted drivers get premium_tier "medium" with base premium 650.
- Anything not covered above defaults to declined with premium_tier "none".

Output: application_status ("accepted"/"declined"), premium_tier, base_premium (number).
