Shipping cost decision for an online store:

Inputs: destination zone as text ("domestic" or "international"), package weight in kg.

Rules:
- Domestic packages cost 5 euros base. Domestic packages heavier than 10 kg cost 12 euros instead.
- International packages cost 20 euros base. International packages heavier than 10 kg cost 45 euros instead.
- Any order total weight above 30 kg is refused regardless of zone: not shippable.

Output: shipping_cost (a number) and status (text). Status must be exactly one of: "shippable", "refused".
